FAQ

Questions

Is HireMate a tool for cheating in live interviews?

No. HireMate is for practice only. It never listens to or helps during a real interview. We make you better; we don't answer for you.

How is my score calculated?

Each answer is scored on structure, relevance, depth and communication using a rubric calibrated with recruiters. Your rank compares you with other HireMate users practising for the same role and level.

Do I need to use my voice?

Voice is recommended because real interviews are spoken, but you can switch to typing if you're somewhere noisy or your connection is poor.

Which roles do you cover?

Product Manager, Software Engineer (behavioural and conceptual, no live coding yet) and Analyst, from fresher to around 5 years of experience.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are monthly or quarterly and you can cancel whenever you like. There are no annual contracts.

What happens to my recordings?

They are used only to generate your feedback, you can delete them at any time, and we never share them with employers.
